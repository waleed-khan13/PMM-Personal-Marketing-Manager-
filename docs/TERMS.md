Google Maps content must not be scraped, exported, cached, or stored except where the applicable agreement permits it. Socium keeps Places search results transient and sends website URLs to the separate public-site crawler only after an operator action.

They must honor opt-outs and suppression records, applicable privacy and marketing law, provider terms, robots directives, copyright, and rate limits.
